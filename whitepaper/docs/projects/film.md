# IDM Film <StatusBadge pillar="film" />

The pillar least connected to the blockchain, and the one with the most
conventional evidence: **two titles already released in national cinemas.**

## Two works, two different roles

The distinction matters, and blurring it would overstate the track record.

| Year | Title | IDM Film's role |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | *Jin Qorin* | **Distributor**, alongside a partner production house |
| 2026 | *Leave No One Behind* | **Production house** |

For the 2026 title, the production house of record is PT IDM Film Sejahtera —
the same legal entity that issues the token. Runtime 74 minutes, released 23
July 2026 across 30 screens of a national cinema chain, spanning Jakarta,
Bogor, Depok, Bandung, Solo, Surabaya, Sidoarjo, and other cities. Additional
distribution routes with two further cinema networks are at agreement stage.

Supporting records — handover documents, the letterheaded cinema list, the
production-house/distributor contract data, the film brief, and the trailer —
exist and can be produced on request.

## Why this pillar is in a token whitepaper

Two honest reasons, and neither is "synergy".

**It is the legal entity's operating history.** A token issuer with a real
operating record, audited by the ordinary demands of cinema distribution, is a
different proposition from an issuer whose only artefact is the token itself.

**It is an audience.** Film reaches people who would never open a crypto
application, and access-based utility can reach them without requiring them to
understand a blockchain.

## Token utility: access, deliberately not funding

There is **no scheme funding films through the token**, and for now that is a
decision rather than a gap.

**What is safe and still meaningful:** the token as **access** — tickets,
premiere screenings, merchandise, behind-the-scenes material. This is
consumption utility. The holder receives a thing, and the thing is the point.

**What is dangerous:** any scheme linking token ownership to film revenue. In
Indonesia, crypto-asset supervision sits with the financial services authority
with an investor-protection focus, and a "buy the token, receive a share of the
film's earnings" structure is an investment contract in substance regardless of
what it is called.

That is not something to be sketched casually in a whitepaper. If such a
structure is ever designed, it will be preceded by legal advice and stated
explicitly — not implied through adjacency.

Until then, this document makes no claim that IDM funds films or shares in
their proceeds.
